Lofi Factory

Lofi Factory is a small personal tool that composes original lofi and folk music with pixel-art scenes and uploads the finished videos to a single YouTube channel owned by its author: @LOFI_MUSIC-o2w.

Every video is reviewed and approved by the channel owner before it is uploaded. The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.
